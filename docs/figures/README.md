They were copied out of it and re-saved here; the preview folder itself was not changed. Every figure is a demonstration or a summary of committed results; the numbers behind them are in `docs/results/`.

## Data files

- `warp_check.json`: copied unchanged from `runs/viz/anatomy/warp_check.json` (written by the `check` step of `scripts/viz_anatomy.py`), SHA-256 `82c9fbe354fbe5c4586ab89d5c7f0b86a201479fbb419eabb4ff97f478329e6d`. - `landmarks.csv`: copied unchanged from `runs/viz/landmarks.csv` (written by `scripts/viz_positions.py`), SHA-256 `e252c0c444a63e086e82379fb6bff057354d61d8e07593dfabe5702e6e278caa`. - `warp_check.csv`: copied unchanged from `runs/viz/anatomy/warp_check.csv` (written by the `check` step of `scripts/viz_anatomy.py`), SHA-256 `6c775f6ecae7e4ed8990e698f27ab4032582e2f28f74703406c6f5b4fc24bebd`. One row per bridged FlyWire DN: the native and warped skeleton checks, whether the DN was used, and our affine against the BANC team's warp in um. Columns: id, cell_type, side, soma, landmark, n_roots_native, nodes_native, nodes_warped, same_tree, nearest_warped_node_um, root_soma_um_nm, root_soma_um_voxel, root_soma_um, root_ok, used, root_vs_root_um, affine_vs_warp_um, dist_from_landmark_centroid_um, diff_x_um, diff_y_um, diff_z_um. Licence as this folder (CC BY-NC 4.0).
- `visual_input.json`: written by `env -u PYTHONPATH python scripts/readme_visual_input.py export` (after its `render` and `gate` steps), SHA-256 `778d0facf8b1eef239bd84ba53c75b5fe07613ea4a89767809a7c02f0c7d551d`. The stage-A visual input for the README figure: what the FlyGym eyes see and how the FlyVis T4/T5 boundary rates respond to the rotating drum. Keys: `provenance`, `window`, `subtypes`, `neurons`, `mean_rate_hz`, `asymmetry`, `lead_in_mean_hz`, `heatmap_vmax_hz`, `eye_images`, `gate`: the render keys with the SHA-256 of each render and of `runs/f5/stageA/boundary_meta.npz`; the input-check window of `scripts/f3b1_common.py`; the T4/T5 subtypes and their neuron counts per eye; the mean rate per render, eye and subtype over that window; the per-subtype asymmetry `a_in` of `scripts/f3b1_brain.py`; the mean rate of the grey lead-in; one shared heatmap colour limit (with the boundary-layer rate clip and the fraction of rates at it); the SHA-256 of the raw eye images of `eye_images()` in `scripts/f5_stageA_preview.py` (recomputed, not stored); and the gate values. The renders behind it (`rot_left_v060_p0`, `rot_right_v060_p0`, `rot_left_v120_p0`, `rot_right_v120_p0`, `static_p0`, `grey`; phase 0) are not committed: `render` re-makes them in `runs/f5/stageA/readme_renders/` by the code path of `scripts/f5_stageA_records.py`, and `gate` showed them identical to the originals (bit for bit against the F5 gate-5 render and the stored FlyVis window means of `scripts/f5_train.py`, and the input-check A_in values equal to `runs/f5/stageA/input_checks.json` and `docs/results/f5_stageA_records.json`). Licence as this folder (CC BY-NC 4.0).

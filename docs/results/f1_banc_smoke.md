## Result

**MN9 is silent (0.00 Hz in every condition and seed) and nothing runs away** (no residual activity after the cut). The network rate equals the rate of the driven GRNs themselves plus a small spread. By the stop rule of the F1 task nothing was recalibrated: any change of `w_syn` for BANC is a pre-registered F2 decision.

Reference, FlyWire v783 with the same protocol (sugar 100 Hz, 1 s; `runs/bisect/openloop_new.json`): MN9 58.5 Hz, network 0.093 Hz.

## Why (read-only diagnostic, `smoke_diag.json`)
- MN9 is [2, 2] hops from the labellar sugar GRNs and [3, 3] hops from the leg sugar GRNs (FlyWire: [2, 2] from the Shiu LB3 GRNs): the path exists.
- MN9 input synapses: BANC [1913.0, 2437.0], FlyWire [5886.0, 5579.0] (3.1x and 2.3x more in FlyWire), consistent with the sparseness of `f1_banc_qa.md` section 3.
- Spread of activity (both groups, seed 0, 1 s): 388 driven neurons at 99 Hz; 188 other neurons spike (4493 spikes), by hop distance {'1': 108, '2': 74, '3': 6, '4': 0, '5': 0}; MN9 spikes 0. The activity dies out after hop 3: propagation is sub-threshold, consistent with a graph sparser than the one `w_syn` was set on (`f1_banc_qa.md` section 3: FlyWire has 2.6x the in-synapses per neuron on the graphs as run).

## Consequence
The registered open-loop tests of F2 (sugar -> MN9, tarsal GRN -> MN9) are expected not to pass on BANC with the unchanged reference parameters. The decision (input normalisation / `w_syn` scaling as a named model variant, or another route) belongs to a pre-registration, with the criterion fixed from open-loop physiology only (CLAUDE.md rule 3), before any BANC closed-loop run.

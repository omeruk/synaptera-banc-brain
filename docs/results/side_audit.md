FlyWire Codex FAQ (codex.flywire.ai/faq): neuron side is decided by soma position; neuropil and neuron side annotations are from the fly's perspective; the FAFB images were left/right inverted during acquisition and all annotations and labels in CAVE and Codex were inverted to correct this.
- **L5.** Bridge on the real DN tables (225 units): drive left -> 152 left / 0 right targets driven (of 152 left); drive right -> 153 right / 0 left (of 153 right).
- **L6.** BANC DN soma side = FlyWire side of `fafb_match`: all DNs 1260 / 1296 (97.2 %), ensemble 298 / 304 (98.0 %). `fafb_nblast_match` (reported only): all 1199 / 1266, ensemble 275 / 292.
- **L7.** Mapping types: b2 consistent, b3 consistent, hg1 consistent, hg2 consistent, hg3 consistent, hg4 consistent, i1 consistent, i2 consistent. DN and MN sides are the same Codex Soma side column of one dataset; the rule of section 1 decides whether an MN's soma side is its muscle side.
- **L8.** Lindsay et al. 2017 (Fig. 1B legend): "The steering muscles on the right side of the fly are imaged"; Lindsay et al. 2017 (Results): "Saccades toward the ipsilateral side—when the wing stroke amplitude on the imaged side decreased". Model: turn_to_amplitudes(turn_bias > 0) -> A_L > A_R, docstring 'turn right'; tests/test_arena_wing.py::test_turn_sign_in_wrench: yaw torque < 0 (right). A_side = A0 + G sum_m s_m r_{m,side}; s_m = effect on the ipsilateral (same-side) amplitude; dA = A_L - A_R > 0 turns right; S > 0 = syndirectional (scripts/f4_design_numbers.py). Lindsay imaged the right side; the signs are stated for the ipsilateral wing, so they apply to both sides under mirror symmetry.

Absolute chirality: a mirror image has the same connectivity, so whether "left" is the fly's left cannot be measured from the connectome. For FlyWire it is documented (Codex FAQ, L4). For BANC no statement was found in the methods text read for this audit; L6 shows that the two datasets' labels agree with each other.

## 2. Limitations recorded before unsealing (prereg/20 section 1)

a. **Budget and learning rate.** `true` and `shuffled` both selected the largest rate of the grid, 0.01, and the validation loss of `shuffled` was still falling at step 2,000. A `true` minus `shuffled` difference at the registered budget may reflect learning speed rather than a difference that would remain with more training or a larger rate.
b. **Turn-sign accuracy is at ceiling.** The `untrained` arm already has validation turn-sign accuracy 0.994, and every trained arm except `bridge_off` reaches 1.000. Samples to set accuracy (0.90) is mostly reached at the first evaluation, so it is limited by the 50-step evaluation interval (800 training trials) and separates the arms little.
c. **`linear` is unstable at every rate.** It diverges at 0.01. At the selected 0.001 its validation loss swings widely between evaluations, so its final-step values, and the final-loss rate choice, are noisy. Its selected checkpoints are not.
d. **The `mlp` rate choice is a near tie.** 0.003 has a lower final validation loss than 0.001 by a small margin, and the `mlp` final-step values are noisy.
e. **`true` is close to the ceiling guard.** On validation, `true` has mean R2 0.975 at the selected checkpoint, just under the prereg/14 section 8.2 ceiling guard of 0.98. The guard needs both arms at or above 0.98, and `shuffled` is at 0.945 (mean validation R2 at the selected checkpoint, as the guard uses on the test set).

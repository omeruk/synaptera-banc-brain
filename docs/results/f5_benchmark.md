# F5 step 2b: GPU support and benchmark

Generated by `scripts/f5_bench_report.py` from `runs/f5/bench/` (not committed). prereg/14_f5_training.md section 10: wall-time-only benchmark, data seed 9999, synthetic inputs; no metric was read and no registered data or training run was made. Evaluation forwards are timed at batch 240 (A) and 8 (B), so the stage-B evaluation cost is an upper bound.

- Machine: x86_64, AMD Ryzen 9 8945HS w/ Radeon 780M Graphics (16 threads, torch 8), NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU (8.00 GiB); torch 2.14.0+cu130, CUDA 13.0, Python 3.10.21. Deterministic algorithms on in every timed step.
- Summary: Serial projection on this machine with the registered chunked edge gradient: stage A 3.6 days, B 8.6 days, C 73.1 days (sddmm, opt-in: 1.0, 2.9, 39.9); stage-A records 0.7 days.

## 1. Safety policy and the first attempt

- The first attempt ran k = 1, 2, ... stage-A training processes side by side on the GPU (NVIDIA driver 596.36). The single-process part before it (`a_gpu done 274 s`) logged no driver event. The driver logged 5,043 nvlddmkm events (Graphics FECS Exception: Logging error 0x2) from 2026-10-17 01:32:15, in the first rounds of the side-by-side test, to 2026-10-17 09:15:21. The rounds k = 1-4 still finished; the round with k = 5 processes hung and the machine had to be powered off. The side-by-side timings were lost; the `a_gpu` results were complete and are used here.
- Policy since then (driver 617.42): one GPU job at a time, no side-by-side GPU processes; each CUDA process capped at 0.7 of the GPU memory (5.60 GiB); every job in a MemoryMax=14G, no swap (systemd scope); pre-checks and a watchdog (`scripts/f5_bench_guard.py`) that kills the job at a GPU temperature of 87 C, low free RAM or a new nvlddmkm event.

## 6. Serial wall-time projection (prereg/14 section 10)

Runs per stage: per arm the 3-rate grid at seed 0 plus the remaining seeds (`true`, `shuffled`, `derange`, `mlp`, `linear` 12 runs each at 10 seeds, 9 at 7; `bridge_off` 7), plus one `untrained` test evaluation. A and B: steps, validation every 50 steps on 240 trials, and the 400-trial test. C: BC and DAgger steps at the stage-C step time, and per run 1,440 learner flights (DAgger, validation, test) at 19.5 s per flight (scene + FlyVis + batch-1 forward); the 960 BC teacher flights once. The `mlp` stage-B/C step is the stage-A one scaled by the trainable count; `linear` is costed as `mlp`.
